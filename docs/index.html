Where AftabPulse sits

Built for operators who already own Huawei SmartLogger 3000 and need a Persian control room on the plant LAN.

vs. FusionSolar cloud

Runs on the operator LAN, talks Modbus directly, and does not depend on the vendor cloud for the live MW figure.

vs. generic SCADA

Fleet map, city MW, honesty rules for stale telemetry, and RTL operations UX instead of a tag browser.

vs. spreadsheet ops

One console for a multi-city portfolio: availability, energy today, and which logger has been quiet.
